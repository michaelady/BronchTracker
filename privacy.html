Privacy policy

Effective date: 14 September 2026

BronchTracker is a parent-facing breathing diary for children (bronchitis flares, asthma exacerbations, wheeze, cough). It is not a medical device, not emergency care, and not a product that sells children’s health data.

If a child has severe breathing distress (cannot speak, blue lips or nails, ribs pulling in, or you are worried), call emergency services (911 in the US) immediately. This website cannot help in an emergency.

What is stored

Child profiles (name, optional birth year, diagnosis tags), episodes, daily check-ins (symptoms, rescue puffs, optional peak flow), medications, action-plan notes you type, and reminder preferences.

  • Guest / local mode: data stays in this browser or on the Android device (SharedPreferences). Nothing is uploaded.
  • Google Sign-In: only if the project owner configured Firebase. Then the diary is stored under your Firebase Auth user in Cloud Firestore so you can sync devices.

What we do not do

  • No advertising in the app or on this GitHub Pages site.
  • No sale of personal information or health information.
  • No data brokers, no third-party marketing SDKs in this MVP.
  • No smart-inhaler or Bluetooth hardware collection.

Children’s data

Profiles describe children but the account is for a parent or caregiver. Do not enter more than you need (first name or nickname is enough). This project is intended as a family diary, not a school or clinic EHR.

Delete

In the app: Kids → Delete all diary data, or delete one child. That clears local storage and, when signed in, the Firestore document for your user. You can also clear site data in the browser or uninstall the Android app.

GitHub Pages

The static Flutter web build is hosted at https://michaelady.github.io/BronchTracker/. GitHub’s hosting logs are governed by GitHub’s own privacy statement. Guest-mode diary entries are not sent to GitHub.
